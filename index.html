    No Last.fm yet?

    Last.fm is a free service that keeps a record of what you listen to. Connect it to Spotify once and it tracks every play from then on.

    1. Create a free account at last.fm/join.
    2. Connect Spotify under Settings, then Applications, at last.fm/settings/applications.
    3. Listen as usual. Come back after a few hours of music and enter your username.

    Want your full history today?

    Request your data from Spotify and load it here. It stays in your browser and arrives by email, usually within a few days to a few weeks.

    1. Open spotify.com/account/privacy and choose Download your data.
    2. Tick Extended streaming history and confirm the email.
    3. When the zip arrives, load it below.

    Questions

    Is my data safe?

    Your cards are saved to your private account, and your Last.fm username is only used to fetch your public top songs. To make trading possible, your in-game name and the cards you own are visible to other signed-in players. Your Google name and email are never shown. A Spotify export is read on your device and never uploaded.

    Why Last.fm and not a Spotify login?

    Spotify limits new apps to a handful of approved users, so a public game can't log everyone in. Last.fm is open, free, and works with a username.

    How is rarity decided?

    By how many people have listened to the song on Last.fm. Fewer listeners means a rarer card. Songs Last.fm can't find count as the most obscure.

    How often can I open packs?

    Each day you get three packs of five cards. The first is Your Listens, drawn from your own top songs. The other two are Discovery packs: real songs from across Last.fm, picked to match the genres and kind of music you play most. Packs refresh at midnight. Once a week (Monday) you also get a Weekly Special with ten random real songs, from Uncommon up to Mythic.

    What are coins and the marketplace?

    Coins let you buy any song in the marketplace. Search for it, check how rare it is, and buy it. The rarer the song, the more it costs, from about 10 coins for a chart-topper to nearly 2,000 for a true underground find. You earn coins from a daily bonus (it grows with your streak), a welcome gift, and by selling songs you got from packs, trades or the market for a quarter of their price. Coins are free and can't be bought with money.

    How does trading work?

    Browse other collectors, pick up to five cards each way, and send an offer. Your cards are held safely while the offer is open. If they accept, the swap happens instantly. If they decline or you cancel, your cards come straight back. It's all free.

    How does the map work?

    Open the Map tab and allow your location. Mystery cards spawn in the real world within 100 miles of where you first opened the map, never closer than about 200 meters to each other and never on water. Each one is swapped for a new one every hour. Walk to a card and get within 60 meters to claim it, or stand right on it (about 10 feet) and it claims itself. If you travel more than 100 miles away, a brand-new batch spawns around you. Rarer cards are scarcer, using nearly the same odds as packs. Your location is only used while the page is open, and it is never saved or shared.

    What are badges?

    Badges are milestones: collecting cards, pulling rare songs, daily streaks, trading, shopping, and exploring the map. Each one you earn pays out free coins straight away, and the Badges tab shows how close you are to the next ones.

    Is this connected to Spotify?

    No. Streamdex is a fan project and isn't affiliated with, or endorsed by, Spotify or Last.fm.
