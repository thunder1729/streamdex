    No Last.fm yet?

    Last.fm is a free service that keeps a record of what you listen to. Connect it to Spotify once and it tracks every play from then on.

    1. Create a free account at last.fm/join.
    2. Connect Spotify under Settings, then Applications, at last.fm/settings/applications.
    3. Listen as usual. Come back after a few hours of music and enter your username.

    Want your full history today?

    Request your data from Spotify and load it here. It stays in your browser and arrives by email, usually within a few days to a few weeks.

    1. Open spotify.com/account/privacy and choose Download your data.
    2. Tick Extended streaming history and confirm the email.
    3. When the zip arrives, load it below.

    Questions

    Is my data safe?

    Your username goes to Last.fm to fetch your public top songs. Your cards and binder are saved in this browser only. A Spotify export is read on your device and never uploaded.

    Why Last.fm and not a Spotify login?

    Spotify limits new apps to a handful of approved users, so a public game can't log everyone in. Last.fm is open, free, and works with a username.

    How is rarity decided?

    By how many people have listened to the song on Last.fm. Fewer listeners means a rarer card. Songs Last.fm can't find count as the most obscure.

    How often can I open packs?

    You start with five packs, and one more arrives every 20 minutes up to five. Cards you discover are yours to keep.

    Is this connected to Spotify?

    No. Streamdex is a fan project and isn't affiliated with, or endorsed by, Spotify or Last.fm.
